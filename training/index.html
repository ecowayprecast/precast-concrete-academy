Important operating terms

Access is controlled and documented.

PCA retains ownership of shared machines and moulds unless a written agreement states otherwise. Admission, production time, utilities, raw materials, storage, sales space and revenue arrangements are confirmed individually.

Structural products require project-specific engineering, approved drawings and verified testing. Incubation does not replace professional design approval or statutory certification.

Payback periods depend on demand, prices, production cost, rejects and sales. PCA does not guarantee a one- or two-month return.
